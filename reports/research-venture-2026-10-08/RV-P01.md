## 3. Meaning of conformance
Conformance is a relationship among an identified requirement set, an identified artifact version, a test method and a recorded result. Exact layout conformance could include page dimensions, margins, fonts, colours, masthead, style definitions, header/footer content, pagination fields and spacing. Semantic publishing conformance includes separate author, contributor, publisher, rights-holder, licence, classification, version, status and evidence identifiers. DOCX structural conformance additionally involves style inheritance, section relationships and field codes. PDF validation assesses the rendered output and metadata; it cannot alone demonstrate DOCX-native structure.
A requirement can be observed as satisfied, observed as violated, not applicable with reason, or not evaluated. Not evaluated must not be silently rewritten as violation. A successful checksum check establishes matching bytes, not satisfaction of the publishing rules. A visually coherent PDF does not independently establish conformity to a specific unseen template.
